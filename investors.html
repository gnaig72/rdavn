  Rising Dragon Associates (RDA) is a Boston-based investment management company investing in growing private and public businesses in Vietnam. Our global business and investment experience combined with a unique local understanding and network enable us to provide maximum value to both our investors and invested companies.

The RDA VIETNAM Fund LP, seeks to achieve capital appreciation and superior long-term returns by making investments in a limited number of opportunities in Vietnam. We focus on defensible business opportunities with superior growth which maximizes return potential while minimizing risk.

 
The opportunities that we pursue have significant demonstrated revenue growth (over 20% yoy), are in defensible businesses and are priced to provide a margin of safety and a favorable risk/reward trade-off. We search across industries and asset classes. We apply strict investment criteria in order to identify and select the best long-term opportunities.

We have over 20 years experience investing in Vietnam. Through the down years of the global financial crises and the Covid era as well as the up years in between and beyond, we have significantly outperformed the benchmark VN-Index.

If you are interested to find out more about investing in Vietnam, please contact us.
