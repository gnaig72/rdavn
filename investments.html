RDA VIETNAM Investments  
  We have been investing in Vietnam since 2007. Our portfolio includes both public and private companies in many sectors including manufacturing, finance, logistics, building materials, and information technology.

We are always looking for growing companies where we can contribute our business expertise and invest equity capital to support solid management teams and good ideas.

 
We are a US-based fund and have built a strong US network in a diverse set of industries. We also have a large international network including business leaders in Europe, India, China, and Singapore. With offices in Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City, our network in Vietnam is quite strong. We have direct expertise in a wide range of industries including information technology, telecom, biotechnology, pharmaceuticals, healthcare, manufacturing, energy, agriculture, and finance.

In the past, we have invested in venture capital start-ups, small leveraged buy-outs, undervalued public companies, and secondary stock in private companies.

If you have an opportunity or situation that you believe would be attractive to us, we encourage you to contact us directly.
